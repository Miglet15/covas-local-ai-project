# Elite Dangerous — COVAS Knowledge Database

## THE GALAXY & SETTING

Elite Dangerous is set in the year 3310 in a 1:1 scale recreation of the Milky Way galaxy,
containing approximately 400 billion star systems. Humanity has colonized the Orion Arm, known
as the "Bubble" — a region roughly 200-500 light years from Sol. The game features real
astronomical data for named stars.

---

## FACTIONS & SUPERPOWERS

### The Three Superpowers
- **Federation** — Democratic superpower based in Sol. Governed by a President and Congress.
  Uses credits, values security and order. Navy is the Federal Navy.
- **Empire** — Monarchical superpower based in Achenar. Ruled by Emperor Arissa Lavigny-Duval.
  Highly class-stratified with a slavery system (Imperial Slaves are indentured, not chattel).
  Navy is the Imperial Navy.
- **Alliance** — Democratic coalition based in Alioth. Governed by the Alliance Assembly and
  Prime Minister. Focused on independence and mutual defense. Navy is the Alliance Defence Force.

### Independent Powers (Powerplay)
Powers include Archon Delaine (pirate lord), Aisling Duval (Imperial princess, anti-slavery),
Denton Patreus (Imperial senator), Edmund Mahon (Alliance Prime Minister), Felicia Winters
(Federal shadow president), Yuri Grom (independent military commander), Zemina Torval
(Imperial senator, pro-slavery), and others.

### Minor Factions
Every system contains minor factions with influence ratings. Faction states include:
Boom, Bust, Civil Unrest, Civil War, Election, Expansion, Famine, Investment, Lockdown,
Outbreak, Piracy, Retreat, and War. Commanders can influence faction states through missions,
trading, bounty hunting, and combat.

## PILOTS FEDERATION

The Pilots Federation is the overarching organization that licenses all independent commanders.
- **Ranks**: Harmless → Mostly Harmless → Novice → Competent → Expert → Master → Dangerous → Deadly → Elite
  (applies separately to Combat, Trade, and Exploration)
- **Pilots Federation District**: Starter systems where new commanders begin
- **Shinrarta Dezhra**: Permit-locked system, home of Jameson Memorial station — accessible
  only to Elite-ranked commanders. Sells all ships and modules at 10% discount.

## SHIP MODULES & ENGINEERING

### Core Modules (every ship has these)
- **Power Plant** — generates power for all ship systems
- **Thrusters** — determines speed and maneuverability
- **Frame Shift Drive (FSD)** — enables supercruise and hyperspace jumps
- **Life Support** — maintains breathable atmosphere; has a timer if breached
- **Power Distributor** — manages SYS/ENG/WEP power pips
- **Sensors** — target lock range and scan capability
- **Fuel Tank** — determines how many jumps before refueling

### Optional Modules (examples)
- Shield Generator / Prismatic Shield Generator (Imperial rank required for Prismatic)
- Hull Reinforcement Package (HRP) — passive hull armor
- Module Reinforcement Package (MRP) — protects modules from damage
- Guardian Shield Reinforcement — hybrid human/Guardian tech shield boost
- Cargo Racks — cargo capacity
- Fuel Scoop — collect hydrogen fuel from scoopable stars (class O, B, A, F, G, K, M — remember: "OBAfgkm")
- Auto Field-Maintenance Unit (AFMU) — repairs other modules in flight
- Detailed Surface Scanner (DSS) — maps planetary surfaces for full exploration value
- Planetary Vehicle Hangar (PVH) — deploys SRV (Surface Recon Vehicle)
- Fighter Hangar — deploys Ship Launched Fighters (SLF)

### Engineering (The Engineers)
Commanders unlock Engineers — reclusive tech specialists who modify modules beyond
standard specifications. Modifications require:
- **Materials**: Raw (elements), Manufactured (alloys/composites), Encoded (data)
- **Grade 1-5**: Higher grades give stronger bonuses but more variance
- **Experimental Effects**: Secondary bonus applied at any grade

Key Engineers and locations:
- Felicity Farseer (Deciat) — FSD upgrades, thrusters
- The Dweller (Wyrd) — power distributor, weapons
- Tod "The Blaster" McQuinn (Wolf 397) — weapons
- Selene Jean (Kuk) — hull reinforcement, armor
- Elvira Martuuk (Long Mu) — FSD, shields
- Professor Palin (Arque) — thrusters (requires 5,000 ly travel)
- Ram Tah (Meene) — sensors, scanners (Guardian tech specialist)

## COMBAT

### Ship Combat Mechanics
- **Power Pips**: 6 pips distributed between SYS (shields), ENG (speed/maneuverability), WEP (weapons)
- **Shield types**: Bi-Weave (fast recharge), Standard, Prismatic (highest capacity, slow recharge)
- **Hull zones**: Canopy, hull integrity — modules can be targeted individually
- **Silent Running**: Disables heat radiation, makes ship harder to scan, but builds heat rapidly

### Weapon Types
- **Kinetic**: Multi-cannons, Cannons, Fragment Cannons — effective vs hull
- **Thermal**: Lasers (Pulse, Burst, Beam) — effective vs shields
- **Explosive**: Missiles, Torpedoes, Mines — bypass shields partially
- **AX Weapons**: Anti-Xeno weapons specifically designed against Thargoid ships
- **Guardian Weapons**: Gauss Cannons, Shard Cannons, Plasma Chargers — highly effective vs Thargoids

### Combat Zones & Bounty Hunting
- **Conflict Zones (CZ)**: Low, Medium, High intensity — fight for either side for war bonds
- **Resource Extraction Sites (RES)**: Asteroid fields where pirates mine; Wanted ships can be hunted
  - Hazardous RES (Haz RES): Most dangerous, highest value targets
- **Bounties**: Scanning a Wanted ship shows its bounty; destroying it pays out at a station in that faction's jurisdiction
- **Notoriety**: Committing crimes raises Notoriety (0-10); must wait for it to decay before paying fines

## EXPLORATION

### Scanning
- **Honk (Full Spectrum Scanner/FSS)**: Jump into system, deploy FSS to identify all bodies
- **Detailed Surface Scanner (DSS)**: Fires probes at planets to reveal surface features and increase payout
- **Efficient Mapping**: Hit all "Mapped By" bonus targets on the DSS probe scanner

### Exploration Payouts
- **Water Worlds, Earth-like Worlds (ELW), Ammonia Worlds**: Highest value bodies
- **First Discovered / First Mapped**: Significant bonus for being first to scan/map a body
- **Cartographic Data**: Sold at Universal Cartographics in station services

### Neutron Stars & White Dwarfs
- **Neutron Highway**: Using a neutron star's jet cone boosts FSD jump range by 4x (one jump)
- **White Dwarf**: Provides 1.5x boost; more dangerous to cone-surf
- **FSD Supercharge**: Requires synthesizing a repair after heavy use; damages FSD

### Notable Locations
- **Sagittarius A\*** — Galactic core supermassive black hole; ~26,000 ly from Sol
- **Colonia** — Independent human colony ~22,000 ly from Sol; has full station services
- **Beagle Point** — One of the farthest reachable points; ~65,000 ly from Sol
- **The Formidine Rift, Hawking's Gap** — mysterious regions with lore significance

## TRADING & ECONOMY

### Commodity Types
- **Metals**: Gold, Platinum, Palladium, Silver — high value, low volume
- **Legal drugs/food**: Lower value, steady demand
- **Rare commodities**: System-specific, value increases with distance sold
- **Technology**: High value manufactured goods
- **Illegal goods**: Narcotics, Slaves (not Imperial), Weapons — high profit, high risk; requires black markets

### Economy Types
- **Extraction**: Mining outputs (metals, minerals)
- **Refinery**: Processes raw materials
- **Industrial**: Manufacturing, machinery
- **High Tech**: Technology, advanced goods
- **Agricultural**: Food, organics
- **Military**: Weapons, combat-related
- **Tourism**: Passenger-related economy
- **Colony**: Newer systems, varied supply/demand

### Station Types
- **Coriolis, Orbis, Ocellus**: Large rotating stations with full services
- **Outposts**: Smaller stations, often no shipyard or large landing pads
- **Planetary Bases**: On planet surfaces; requires landing on planet
- **Fleet Carriers**: Player-owned mobile stations, 500 ly jump range

### Trading Tools (for Commander reference)
- **Inara.cz**: Comprehensive player database — ships, engineers, commanders, markets
- **Spansh.co.uk**: Galaxy map tools, neutron plotter, trade router, fleet carrier planner
- **EDDB.io**: Market and station database (legacy, partially maintained)

## MINING

### Mining Methods
- **Core Mining**: Find asteroids with glowing fissures; use Seismic Charge Launcher and Blast Charges to crack open
- **Laser Mining**: Use Mining Lasers to chip fragments; collect with Collector Limpets
- **Sub-Surface Mining**: Use Sub-Surface Displacement Missiles to extract deposits below surface

### Mining Equipment
- Mining Laser, Seismic Charge Launcher, Abrasion Blaster, Sub-Surface Displacement Missile
- Collector Limpet Controller — autonomous collection of fragments
- Prospector Limpet Controller — scan asteroids to find best yield
- Refinery — processes raw fragments into sellable commodities

### High Value Mining Targets
- **Low Temperature Diamonds (LTDs)**: Found in icy rings; extremely high value
- **Void Opals**: Found in icy rings; very high value
- **Alexandrite, Musgravite, Monazite, Serendibite**: Rare minerals, high value

## THE THARGOIDS

### Overview
The Thargoids are an insectoid alien species, humanity's primary non-human threat.
They are highly aggressive and have attacked human space in cycles. The current conflict
is known as the Second Thargoid War (ongoing as of 3310).

### Thargoid Ships
- **Scout**: Small, fast attack craft. Vulnerable to AX weapons.
- **Interceptor variants** (in order of difficulty):
  - Cyclops — 1 heart
  - Basilisk — 2 hearts, faster than Cyclops
  - Medusa — 3 hearts
  - Hydra — 4 hearts, most dangerous interceptor
- **Titan**: Massive mothership-class vessel; requires coordinated fleet attacks

### Fighting Thargoids
- **AX weapons** required (or Guardian weapons): Standard human weapons deal minimal damage
- **Hearts**: Must be exposed by depleting the Thargoid's outer shield, then damaged while glowing
- **Caustic Missiles**: Thargoids fire caustic missiles that do ongoing hull damage; use Caustic Sink Launchers
- **EMP Pulse**: Thargoids emit an EMP on interdiction that shuts down ship systems temporarily
  — install Shutdown Field Neutralizer to resist
- **Anti-Xeno Initiative (AXI)**: Player group specializing in Thargoid combat

### Thargoid Sites
- **Barnacles**: Organic structures where Thargoids harvest Meta-Alloys
- **Thargoid Surface Sites**: Ancient alien structures on planet surfaces
- **Maelstroms**: Massive Thargoid anomalies that appeared in human space; source of Titan attacks

## THE GUARDIANS

The Guardians are an extinct alien civilization that preceded humanity by millions of years.
Their ruins are found across the galaxy.

- **Guardian Technology**: Blueprints for Guardian-human hybrid modules — weapons, FSD boosters,
  hull reinforcements, and power plants
- **Guardian FSD Booster**: Increases jump range significantly; highly recommended for exploration
- **Ram Tah**: The Engineer specializing in Guardian technology (Meene system)
- **Guardian Sites**: Found via Ram Tah missions or community discovery

## POWERPLAY

Powerplay is an ongoing meta-game where commanders pledge to a Power and work to expand
their territory, undermine rivals, and earn Power-specific rewards.

- **Cycle**: Resets weekly (Thursday)
- **Merits**: Earned by trading, combat, and missions in controlled/contested systems
- **Ranks 1-5**: Higher rank unlocks Power-specific modules (e.g., Prismatic Shields for Empire)
- **Preparation / Expansion / Fortification / Undermining**: Four core Powerplay activities

---

## COMMUNITY GOALS (CGs)

Community Goals are limited-time events where the entire player base contributes to a
shared objective (delivering goods, turning in bounties, etc.) for tiered rewards.
They typically run 1-2 weeks and are posted at Galnet News in-game and on the official site.

## CONTROLS & MECHANICS

### Flight
- **Supercruise**: FSD-powered travel within a star system; throttle to 75% "blue zone" for optimal approach
- **Hyperspace**: Jumping between star systems; requires FSD charged and destination selected
- **Docking**: Request docking permission at stations; auto-dock available with Docking Computer module
- **Silent Running**: Toggle heat venting; useful for evading scans, dangerous if overheated

### Pips (Power Distribution)
- 6 pips total, moved between SYS / ENG / WEP
- **4 SYS**: Maximum shield regeneration and resistance
- **4 ENG**: Maximum speed and boost recharge
- **4 WEP**: Maximum weapon capacitor recharge

### Heat Management
- Ships generate heat from thrusters, weapons, FSD charging
- **Heat Sinks**: Expendable modules that dump heat instantly
- Exceeding ~100% heat damages modules; critical above 150%

### Synthesis
Commanders can synthesize limited repairs, ammo, and boosts using raw materials:
- FSD Injection (basic/standard/premium): Increases jump range for one jump
- Heat Sink Ammo, Heatsink, AFMU Repairs, SRV Ammo, etc.
